Static Electricity

Question 1

Define: Electricity

Answer:
Electricity is a physical phenomenon related to electric charges and their interactions.
الترجمة:
الكهربية: ظاهرة فيزيائية تتعلق بالشحنات الكهربية وتفاعلاتها.
Question 2

Define: Electric field

Answer:
Electric field is a region around an electric charge where its effect on other charges is observed.
الترجمة:
المجال الكهربي: منطقة حول الشحنة الكهربية يظهر فيها تأثيرها على الشحنات الأخرى.
Question 3

Define: The electric capacitor

Answer:
The electric capacitor is a device consisting of two conductors isolated from each other to store electric charges.
الترجمة:
المكثف الكهربي: جهاز يتكون من موصلين معزولين عن بعضهما لتخزين الشحنات الكهربية.
Question 4

Define: The capacitance C of a capacitor

Answer:
Capacitance is a physical quantity that measures the ability of a capacitor to store electric charges.
الترجمة:
السعة الكهربية: كمية فيزيائية تقيس قدرة المكثف على تخزين الشحنات الكهربية.
Question 5

Define: The Farad

Answer:
The Farad is the unit of measurement of electric capacitance (F).
الترجمة:
الفاراد: وحدة قياس السعة الكهربية (F).
Question 6

Define: Electrometer

Answer:
Electrometer is a device used to measure electric potential (not electric field intensity).
الترجمة:
الكهربائي: جهاز يستخدم لقياس الجهد الكهربي (وليس شدة المجال الكهربي).
Question 7

Define: Conductors

Answer:
Conductors are materials that allow electric current to pass through easily (such as copper and aluminum).
الترجمة:
الموصلات: مواد تسمح بمرور التيار الكهربي بسهولة (مثل النحاس والألومنيوم).
Question 8

Define: Semi-conductors

Answer:
Semi-conductors are materials whose conductivity is between conductors and insulators (such as silicon and germanium).
الترجمة:
أشباه الموصلات: مواد توصيليتها بين الموصلات والعوازل (مثل السيليكون والجرمانيوم).
Question 9

Define: Insulators

Answer:
Insulators are materials that do not allow electric current to pass through (such as glass and rubber).
الترجمة:
العوازل: مواد لا تسمح بمرور التيار الكهربي (مثل الزجاج والمطاط).
Question 10

Define: Static Electricity

Answer:
Static Electricity is the accumulation of electric charges on the surface of objects without movement.
الترجمة:
الكهربية الساكنة: تجمع الشحنات الكهربية على سطح الأجسام دون حركة.
Question 11

Define: Dynamic Electricity

Answer:
Dynamic Electricity is the movement of electric charges in a closed path (electric current).
الترجمة:
الكهربية المتحركة: حركة الشحنات الكهربية في مسار مغلق (التيار الكهربي).
Question 12

الترجمة:
خطأ - الكهربائي يقيس الجهد الكهربي (V)، وليس شدة المجال الكهربي (E).

Question 28

Two charges are separated by 3 cm. Object A has a charge of +6 μC, while object B has a charge of -3μ C. What is the force on object A?

Answer:
F = k × |q₁ × q₂| / r² F = 9×10⁹ × |6×10⁻⁶ × (-3×10⁻⁶)| / (0.03)² F = 180 N (Attraction)
Question 29

Two identical charges exert a force of 0.9 N at 1 m apart. Find the magnitude of each charge.

Answer:
q² = F × r² / k q² = 0.9 × 1² / (9×10⁹) q² = 10⁻¹⁰ q = 10⁻⁵ C = 10 μC
Question 30

What must be the distance between point charge q1 = 26 μC and point charge q2 = -47 μC for the electrostatic force between them to have a magnitude of 5.7 N?

Answer:
r² = k × |q₁ × q₂| / F r² = 9×10⁹ × |26×10⁻⁶ × (-47×10⁻⁶)| / 5.7 r² ≈ 1.93 r ≈ 1.39 m
Question 31

Find the distance where two 1 C charges exert a force equal to a person's weight (700N)

Answer:
r² = k × q₁ × q₂ / F r² = 9×10⁹ × 1 × 1 / 700 r² ≈ 12.857×10⁶ r ≈ 3585 m ≈ 3.6 km
Question 32

Find the equivalent capacitance of the five-capacitor network shown in the opposite Figure. (3 μF, 11 μF, 12 μF, 6 μF, 9 μF)

Answer:
C₁ = 3 + 11 = 14 μF (parallel) C₂ = 12 + 6 = 18 μF (parallel) 1/C₃ = 1/14 + 1/18 = 32/252 C₃ = 7.875 μF (series) C_total = 7.875 + 9 = 16.875 μF
Question 33

A force of 2 N is acting on the charge 4 μ C at any point. Calculate the electric field intensity at that point.

Answer:
E = F / q E = 2 / (4×10⁻⁶) E = 0.5×10⁶ N/C E = 5×10⁵ N/C

Dynamic Electricity

Question 34

Define: Electric current

Answer:
Electric current is the movement of electric charges in a closed path.
الترجمة:
التيار الكهربي: حركة الشحنات الكهربية في مسار مغلق.
Question 35

Define: Conventional Current

Answer:
Conventional Current is the direction of movement of positive charges (from positive to negative).
الترجمة:
التيار التقليدي: اتجاه حركة الشحنات الموجبة (من الموجب إلى السالب).
Question 36

Define: Actual Current

Answer:
Actual Current is the movement of electrons (from negative to positive).
الترجمة:
التيار الفعلي: حركة الإلكترونات (من السالب إلى الموجب).
Question 37

Define: The electric current intensity

Answer:
Electric current intensity is the amount of charge that passes through a cross-section in one second.
الترجمة:
شدة التيار الكهربي: كمية الشحنة التي تمر في مقطع في الثانية الواحدة.
Question 38

Define: Ampere

Ampere is the unit of measurement of electric current intensity (A).
الترجمة:
الأمبير: وحدة قياس شدة التيار الكهربي (A).

Question 62

Question 63

Answer:

Question 64

100W heating element is connected to a 25 V power supply voltage. Calculate a) the resistance of the element when it is hot b) the current drawn from the supply

Answer:
a) R = V² / P R = 625 / 100 R = 6.25 Ω b) I = P / V I = 100 / 25 I = 4 A
Question 65

If 60 J of energy are used to move 2 C, what is the potential difference?

Answer:
V = W / Q V = 60 / 2 V = 30 V
Question 66

A copper wire 30 m long and 2×10⁻⁶ m² cross sectional area has a potential difference of 3 V across. Calculate the current if the copper resistivity is 1.79 × 10⁻⁸ Ω.m

Answer:
R = ρ × L / A R = (1.79×10⁻⁸ × 30) / (2×10⁻⁶) R = 0.2685 Ω I = V / R I = 3 / 0.2685 I ≈ 11.18 A
Question 67

A metallic wire is 1 m long and 1mm² in cross -sectional area. It carries a current of intensity 4A when a 2V potential difference is applied between its ends. Calculate the conductivity of the metallic wire

Answer:
R = V / I R = 2 / 4 R = 0.5 Ω ρ = R × A / L ρ = 0.5 × 1×10⁻⁶ / 1 ρ = 0.5×10⁻⁶ Ω.m σ = 1 / ρ σ = 1 / (0.5×10⁻⁶) σ = 2×10⁶ S/m
Question 68

Find the voltage across a 5 Ω resistor when 2 A flows through it.

Answer:
V = I × R V = 2 × 5 V = 10 V
Question 69

A 45V potential difference is placed across a 5 Ω resistor and a 10 Ω resistor connected in series. Calculate a) the equivalent resistance of the circuit b) the current through the circuit c) the voltage drop across each resistor

Answer:
a) R_total = 5 + 10 = 15 Ω b) I = 45 / 15 = 3 A c) V₁ = 3 × 5 = 15 V V₂ = 3 × 10 = 30 V
Question 70

Three resistors of 60 Ω, 30 Ω, and 20 Ω are connected in parallel across a 90V difference in potential. Calculate a) the equivalent resistance of the circuit b) the current in the circuit (c) the current through each branch of the circuit.

Answer:
a) 1/R_total = 1/60 + 1/30 + 1/20 = 6/60 R_total = 10 Ω b) I_total = 90 / 10 = 9 A c) I₁ = 90/60 = 1.5 A I₂ = 90/30 = 3 A I₃ = 90/20 = 4.5 A
Question 71

The opposite figure shows a simple electric circuit, calculate: a) The total resistance of the circuit b) The total current flowing through the battery, assuming the battery has no internal resistance. c) The current flowing through 1 Ω resistor

Answer:
a) R_parallel = 5/6 Ω R_total = 5/6 + 3 = 23/6 ≈ 3.83 Ω b) I_total = 4 / (23/6) ≈ 1.04 A c) I₁ ≈ 0.87 A
